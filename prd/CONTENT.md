# Elite Weavers Content Model

## Categories
Banarasi, Kanjivaram, Chanderi, Paithani, Patola, Bandhani, Tussar, Organza, Linen, Cotton, Silk, Handloom, Wedding, Festive, Party / Occasion.

## Collections
- **The Night Loom:** deep indigo Banarasi and evening silk.
- **Southern Light:** jewel-toned Kanjivaram with temple borders.
- **Barely There:** Chanderi, organza, and linen for light drape.
- **The Celebration Edit:** wedding, festive, and occasion pieces.
- **Everyday Heirlooms:** cotton, tussar, and handloom staples.

## Product structure
`id`, `name`, `slug`, `category`, `fabric`, `weave`, `origin`, `color`, `occasion`, `price`, `compareAt`, `tag`, `description`, `care`, `delivery`, `images[]`, `rating`, `reviewCount`, `isNew`, `isBestSeller`.

## Sample voice
Plain, sensory, and specific. “A midnight Banarasi with a quiet gold geometry through the pallu.” Avoid inflated claims and invented technical precision.

## Editorial content
- A border is a map of the hand that made it.
- In Kanchipuram, silk is still measured in patience.
- How to fold, store, and air a silk saree.
- Five ways to bring handloom into an evening wardrobe.

## Testimonials
Use short, attributed quotes from realistic shoppers, capped at three lines. Example: “The color is even more beautiful in natural light. It feels special without feeling precious.” - Meera S.

## Image direction
Use real textile and portrait photography with varied crops: full drape, border detail, loom hand, portrait, and flat-lay. Keep product imagery bright enough to judge color and avoid repetitive placeholders.
