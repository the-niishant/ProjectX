# Elite Weavers Pages

## Home `/`
Announcement, responsive header, cinematic hero, brand introduction, featured collections, category explorer, trending product row, best sellers, new arrivals, heritage story, editorial showcase, fabric and occasion links, testimonials, social gallery, newsletter, and premium footer. Homepage filters and product cards remain usable without leaving the page.

## Collections `/collections`
Editorial collection hero; category, fabric, color, occasion, and price filters; sort; product grid; loading, empty, and error states; persistent wishlist and cart actions.

## Collection `/collections/:slug`
Collection-specific palette and story, regional motif, curated hero, product grid, related craft story, and recommendations.

## Product `/products/:slug`
Gallery with thumbnails and zoom affordance; name, price, material, weave, origin, craftsmanship, quantity, wishlist, add to cart, buy now, delivery and care accordions, reviews, related products, and frequently bought together.

## Wishlist `/wishlist`
Saved pieces grid with remove controls, add-to-bag actions, empty state, and sign-in or email-save prompt placeholder.

## Cart `/cart`
Line items, quantity controls, removal, promo code, shipping threshold message, subtotal, and checkout CTA. On desktop this is a drawer; on mobile it becomes a full-height sheet.

## Checkout `/checkout`
Contact, shipping, delivery, payment, validation, order summary, promo state, and simulated success confirmation. Error and loading states are inline.

## Heritage `/heritage`
Weaver portraits, regional craft stories, material education, and a transparent sourcing narrative.

## Journal `/journal`
Editorial articles on borders, drape, care, and contemporary ways to wear heritage textiles.

## Responsive requirements
Desktop supports hover image swap and wide editorial compositions. Tablet reduces grids and keeps side-by-side stories where legible. Mobile uses a bottom-safe header, horizontal scroll rows, stacked product details, full-height drawers, and 44px minimum touch targets.
